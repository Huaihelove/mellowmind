## Description

Mellow Mind is a calm, local-first wellness app for people who want a softer way to check in with themselves.

Use playful personality references to notice patterns in how you process emotions. Draw a mood card when you need a gentle pause. Shake the wish tube, choose a wish slip by hand, and turn a vague feeling into one small act of self-care. Check the Daily Emotional Almanac for a low-pressure suggestion, or open the Healing Space to journal privately, sort a worry, or listen to bundled relaxation audio.

CORE FEATURES

• Multi-dimensional personality profile
Choose your blood type, birth date, and birth year to receive a personality-reflection profile based on blood type, Western zodiac, and Chinese zodiac traditions. The profile focuses on present strengths, emotional needs, boundaries, and ways to unwind.

• Gentle mood cards
Hold to gather energy, shuffle by hand, and choose a card yourself. Each reflection includes three fixed sections: your current emotional state, a thought you may be overlooking, and a kind self-care suggestion. A swipe interaction softens the card and reveals a daily emotional keyword.

• Healing wish slips
Press your palms together, shake the wish tube, and draw a slip by hand. Five positive themes—Calm, Heal, Ease, Flow, and Tender—offer emotional comfort and encouragement for the present moment.

• Wish wall, sealed wishes, and check-ins
Leave a wish anonymously or by name, add a warm comment, or like a post. Sealed wishes are encrypted locally and can be reopened after 7 or 30 days. Daily check-ins help you notice small achievements without pressure or comparison.

• Daily Emotional Almanac
See a relaxation index, overthinking-avoidance guidance, small decompression ideas, a Chinese-zodiac mindset reminder, a personality-strength suggestion, and a blood-type emotional-care tip. Content refreshes daily.

• Healing Space
Write privately in Tree Hollow and receive a gentle local reply. Use the Anxiety Sorter to break a worry into present-focused pieces. Listen to four bundled relaxation soundscapes. Save cards, wish slips, almanac entries, and reflections in your personal collection.

• Personalization and skins
Daily check-ins and accumulated wishes unlock growth skins. Premium card-face and wish-tube skin packs are available as one-time purchases. An optional premium daily emotional-care report is available as an auto-renewing monthly subscription.

PRIVACY AND SAFETY

• No account required
• Local-first storage with SwiftData
• Sealed wishes encrypted with AES-GCM; key held in iOS Keychain
• No ad SDKs, splash ads, pop-up ads, or cross-app tracking
• In-app controls to clear profile data, wishes, journal entries, saved cards, check-ins, and skins
• Notifications are optional and requested only for sealed-wish reminders
• Motion is read only on the wish-tube screen to recognize an intentional shake; motion history is never stored or uploaded
• Audio is bundled locally and does not require microphone or media-library permission

## App Review Information — Notes

Thank you for reviewing Mellow Mind.

POSITIONING

Mellow Mind is a mood-journal, personality-reflection, self-care, and stress-relief app. It is designed as a wellness tool, not a divination or fate-judgment product. Blood type, Western zodiac, Chinese zodiac, tarot-inspired cards, and wish slips are used only as playful self-reflection prompts and present-moment emotional support. The content makes no claims about future events, outcomes, wealth, relationships, health, or luck.

The fixed footer on every reflection surface reads:

"Disclaimer: All content is for personality reference and emotional comfort only. It is not a fortune-telling service and cannot predict the future."

PRIVACY

- No account or login is required.
- Profile data, wishes, sealed messages, journal entries, check-ins, saved cards, and skin choices are stored locally with SwiftData.
- Sealed wishes use AES-GCM; the key is stored in iOS Keychain.
- There are no ad SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.
- The app includes settings to clear all local data or clear only wish/journal content.
- App Store purchases are processed by Apple StoreKit; the app does not collect payment details.
- Motion is accessed only while the wish-tube screen is active, to recognize shaking. Motion data is not stored or uploaded.
- Notifications are optional and requested only for sealed-wish reminders.
- Audio is bundled in the app; no microphone or media-library access is used.

COMMUNITY AND MODERATION

This version does not provide a networked public community. Wish-wall content is local-only and is not uploaded or shown to other real users. Sample posts are labeled as local examples. The app provides length limits, gentle-language filtering, report, hide, and delete controls in preparation for safety expectations, but no server-side public UGC exists.

PURCHASES

The app uses StoreKit 2. Both the skin store and settings expose Restore Purchases. Subscription details, renewal terms, cancellation information, and Manage Subscription are visible in the app. The premium report is an emotional self-care feature and makes no medical or outcome claims.

HEALTH AND SAFETY

Tree Hollow, the Anxiety Sorter, the Daily Emotional Almanac, and the premium report provide emotional companionship and self-reflection only. They do not diagnose, treat, or replace professional care. Crisis guidance directs users to local emergency services, crisis lines, or a trusted person.

No login credentials are needed for review. Use StoreKit sandbox testing for purchases.
